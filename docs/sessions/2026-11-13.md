## Decisions
- No decisions recorded; these are proposals awaiting the owner's choices.
  - **Fill scale:** equal-area by default, with linear as an option.
  - **Segments:** at most about three per wedge.
  - **Network:** download/upload halves on a log scale from 1 KB/s to 1 GB/s.
  - **Disk:** capacity plus an I/O rim, or a read/write activity mode.
  - **Power:** stacked components against a fixed maximum per Mac.
  - **Temperature:** hottest sensor from 30 to 100 °C, on a colour ramp tied to radius whose
    lightness rises with heat; the wedge is tinted by thermal state.
  - **Battery:** bolt for charging, amber plug for not charging, plug for full on power, no mark
    on battery; pulse off by default.
  - **Size:** presets tied to the text that fits each one, so text never shrinks.
  - **History:** no sparkline along the arc; use 60-second peak ticks instead.
- Code findings that matter for gauges, recorded in the TASKS backlog item:
  - `PowerSample.loadPercent` divides by a session peak that only ever rises.
  - `NetworkSample.activityPercent` caps at 12.5 MB/s.
  - `MemorySample.pressurePercent` is used ÷ total, not kernel memory pressure.
